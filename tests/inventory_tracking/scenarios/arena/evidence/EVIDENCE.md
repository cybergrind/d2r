# Evidence for the refit of the arena's monsters

Generated by `uv run --offline python -m tests.inventory_tracking.scenarios.arena.evidence.report` from the takes under `inventory_tracking/runs/combat`; do not edit by hand. `evidence.json` beside this file holds the same measures per type for a program to read; `split.json` names the takes of each half. The method is in the module texts of `blows.py` (who a drop is put on) and `rates.py` (what is counted); the model's numbers are read off its parameters by `model.py`.

- **Takes:** 748 found, 674 read (schema 2), 74 left out (74 schema 1: no vitals).
- **Split:** by take name (the first byte of sha1(take name): even is train, odd is check): **train 330 takes, 9,730 s, 810 drops, 33,316 points; check 344 takes, 9,367 s, 698 drops, 36,360 points.** The refit is to be fitted on train and judged on check; every number that can become a parameter is given for both. Takes of one game session fall in both halves, so the halves are not independent of each other in area and monster mix: check guards against fitting noise, not against a different zone.
- **A drop** is a fall of the character's life of 5 points or more between two read frames (combat/hurts.py `lost`: a fall of the most life with it is no blow), counted across frames whose vitals were not read.
- **Sample sizes are small.** The takes hold 1,508 drops in 5.3 hours; 107 are a melee blow of one monster alone and 83 a shot of one alone. Every number below has its n; a per-type number on under 10 is an anecdote.

## 1. What the takes can and cannot say

- **Whose a missile is: read, not inferred.** The missile's unit record holds its owner's unit id. Of the hostile missiles tracked (30,433), 30,307 belong to a monster the take saw and 126 to none it saw. Neither the type's `MissA1` (not in the bundled tables) nor co-occurrence is needed; where the installed game's monstats names a type's missile the owner id agrees with it (blows.py).
- **Whom a monster is after:** the path record's target, in 574 of 674 takes. Without it only a monster beside the character (4.5 units) is a striker.
- **Lines of sight:** the level file's flight layer, in 653 of 674 takes; closed doors are ignored.
- **Enchantments:** in the take since 2026-10-11 22:23 UTC, else from the terror probe logs (40,670 of 40,928 units known).
- **Not in a take:** the animation frame of a swing (a swing is a run of frames in one attack mode); whether a blow missed, was blocked or found the character gone; poison and other damage over time (no state list of the character); the damage kind of a drop; the character's resistances before 2026-10-11 22:23 UTC (169 of the 1,508 drops carry them, so no table here is split by resistance; the rows in the cache hold them).
- **A drop's suspects:** a hostile in an attack mode beside the character or within 12 units with the character as its target; the owners of the hostile missiles within 3 units (or within 6 and gone by the next frame); a dead owner makes it a death's missile; with no suspect, an enchanted or exploding hostile that died within 8 units in the 15 frames before.

## 10. What the refit should change, in order of the life it explains

Ordered by the points of the train half each line carries (said at its head). Each line: the measured number on train and on check, the model's, the sample, the expected effect and how far to trust it.

1. **What is not a blow at all** (12,333 points on the train half, 37% of its life lost, in 538 drops of a median 10). No suspect: 454 of them with nothing hostile within 8 units. 308 (3,112 points) come in runs 0.4 s apart: damage over time. 88 (3,089 points) are 2% of the most life each, many in the first seconds of a take with nobody near: a cost the character pays itself, by the look of it, not a monster's doing. Expected effect on the refit: none on the blows, and that is the point: a refit that matches a take's total life lost with blows alone makes the blows carry this share too. Judge the refit on the drops with a suspect, or model damage over time apart. Confidence: high on the size, none on the causes (a take holds no state list of the character).
2. **Melee: a swing is not a hit** (7,726 points on the train half: the sole melee drops and the drops whose suspects are all swings; 1,734 more are swings with missiles). Train, the melee types that fight: 725 swings begun within 3.5 units in 668 monster-seconds there: 1.08 a second (check 1.01). Model: 1.1 to 1.6 by type; the measured rate is 0.56 to 0.90 of the model's for the 5 types with enough time, so **the swing rate is not what is wrong**. What is: 0.29 of the attack runs are cut short before the middle of the swing (check 0.27; the model finishes every one), and of the 433 whole swings with the character as the target 0.07 to 0.20 took life (check 0.05 to 0.26 of 413); with nothing else hostile near, 0.25 of 61 (check 0.16 of 38); with the character standing still, 0.09 to 0.24 of 292 (check 0.07 to 0.33 of 261). Model: 1.00. In life per second a monster stands in reach the takes show 0.04 to 0.18 of the model's, by type. Expected effect: a chance of about 0.2 that a whole swing takes life and runs cut short at the measured share bring a monster in contact to about a seventh of what it deals now (0.2 x 0.7); whom it goes for and the size of a blow (the lines below) make up the rest of the gap. Confidence: high on the direction and the order of size (both halves, every type); low per type (19 to 92 whole swings each on the train half). What the takes cannot split: a miss by the to-hit roll, a block, Psychic Ward taking the blow, and the character having left the reach (the stood-still figure bounds the last).
3. **Whom a monster goes for** (it scales the melee line). Train: with a companion nearer than the character, the path record named the character 0.44 of the time (2,798 s against 3,606 s on a companion; check 0.44); with the character nearer, 0.77 (check 0.79). Model: 0 and 1 (the nearest of all), 1 and 1 for the Goatman AI. By kind the two go opposite ways (the per-type table): melee types name the character about half the time when a companion is nearer and nine times in ten when it is not; archers name a companion almost always, whoever is nearer, which the missiles confirm (the ranged line). Expected effect: more melee on the character than the nearest-of-all rule gives where companions stand in front (the scenes CALIBRATION.md found under half), fewer arrows. Confidence: high (thousands of monster-seconds, the halves agree to 0.02).
4. **A Cold Enchanted monster's death nova (`coldunique`, 194): not in the model** (4,824 points on the train half, 14% of all its life lost). Train: 24 drops with the dead one's nova near and nobody striking, median 179, largest 403, from a corpse 8.1 units off at the median and 11.7 at the most. Check: 32 drops, median 186, largest 360, corpse 8.3 units off at the median. Model: 0. Who lets it go (all takes, deaths within 20 units): 160 of 160 deaths of an elite carrying Cold Enchanted showed the missiles and 58 were followed by a drop put on them; of 844 deaths of plain monsters and minions carrying it, 29 and 7. Expected effect: the single largest named source of big drops (see the list of drops over 300). Confidence: high that it exists, on who lets it go and on its size (the owner id ties the missile to the corpse; the drops cluster near 180 and near twice that); the chance that a nova reaching the character takes life is the deaths table's `put on it` over `missile`. A Fire Enchanted death's explosion (`fireexplosion`, 82) is seen and takes a median of under 10 points: negligible with fire resistance at the cap.
5. **Ranged: the shot rate, whom the shot is at, and what ends on the character** (3,818 points on the train half: sole ranged drops and drops between several missiles). Train, the threat table's ranged types: 2,282 shots in 5,316 s in sight within 30 units: 0.43 a second (check 0.42; model 1). 0.62 of the missiles flew at where the character stood (check 0.66), 0.27 ended on it (check 0.24) and 0.33 on a companion (check 0.33). Of the 728 that ended on it 0.02 to 0.17 took life (check 0.02 to 0.20 of 542); per shot 0.010 to 0.077 (check 0.007 to 0.071; model 1.0). Per second in sight, an arena shooter whose nearest target is the character takes life 30 to 233 times as often as a shooter of the takes. Moving: of the missiles let go at where the character stood, 0.50 ended on it when it stood (1,145 shots) and 0.23 when it teleported meanwhile (431). A hit, when it comes, is larger than the model's shot: 11 of 56 sole ranged drops (all takes) exceed the model's most for that monster, the largest 5.31 times. Expected effect: this is the largest single correction to the arena (CALIBRATION.md has 392 of its 688 blows as shots). Confidence: high on the rate and on where missiles end (thousands of shots, halves agree); medium on the share that takes life (tens of drops); low on the points of a hit per type (the table: under 10 sole hits each).
6. **Reach** (770 points on the train half came from further than 4.5 units). Sole melee blows came from 3.1 units at the median and 5.1 at the 90th percentile (check 3.2 and 6.6; model 3.2). The reach curve: swings are begun at 1.07 to 1.07 a second per monster standing 2 to 3.5 units off, 0.54 at 3.5 to 4 and 0.30 at 4 to 5; of the 220 whole swings at the character begun from 4 units and further 0.04 to 0.10 took life. So 3.2 is right for the body of the blows; beyond it a monster begins a swing ever less often (the curve) and still takes life now and then: by type and rank, a few blows each (section 4; hurts.py has the Herald of Tower Cellar 5 striking from 7.7). Confidence: medium for the body, low for the tail.
7. **The big single drops.** 20 drops over 300 points in all takes (11 train, 9 check), 8,328 points; by class: 8 several, 6 nova/explosion, 3 unknown, 2 sole melee, 1 sole ranged. Beside the cold nova: (a) several blows landing between two frames (the `several` rows of the list), which right rates give by themselves only if single blows are as large as the takes'; (b) single blows over the model's most: 13 of 103 sole melee drops (all takes) exceed the heaviest blow the model lets that very monster deal, the largest 3.46 times (Ice Spawn 458: 320 and 322 points against 93). A blow's top is too low for some types, which no rate mends. Confidence: medium; the list in section 8 is the evidence, and the refit should be shown to produce drops of this size at about this rate (20 in 19,097 s).

## 11. What looked wrong on the way (nothing was changed)

- **sim/monsters.py reads `aidel` and never uses it.** `Traits.delay` is set from `aidel(H)` and no line of `Pack` looks at it: a monster thinks again the frame its strike or its stand is over. The swing rate that gives is still close to the takes' within 3.5 units (section 5), so this is a note, not a finding.
- **STRIKE_FRAMES 16 and HIT_FRAME 8 are one number for all types; the takes show a swing's length by type and mode** (section 5, `usual frames`: 10 to 24 frames).
- **0.32 of the attack runs within reach are cut short** before the middle of the swing (hit recovery or death under the blades, as far as a take can say). The model has no hit recovery (its module text says so): its monsters finish every swing.
- **hurts.py `target_unit` reads the target's id and not its unit type** (the dword before it: 0 a player, 1 a monster). `aimed` compares the id with the character's alone. Not seen to matter; the type is there to be read (blows.py `target_of`).
- **hurts.py `hurts` starts again after a frame whose vitals were not read**, so a drop across such a frame is not a row; calibrate.py `life_lost` counts it. blows.py counts across, as calibrate does.
- **hurts.py `missile_point` raises on a missile row with an empty path record** (struct.error); such rows exist (4 of 125 takes sampled hold some). `hurts` only reads the missiles of the frame before a drop and has not met one; blows.py passes over them.
- **units.jsonl does not hold the companions at all**: the mercenary and the pets are only in the frames' rows (`owner`, `ally`), which is where blows.py `companions` takes them from.
- **The recorder writes no first sight for some of the character's own missiles** (20261012T165857Z-115: 420 frame rows of `echoingstrike` and 67 of `echoingstrike_detonate` whose unit ids missiles.jsonl never names). blows.py takes such a missile for the character's when every first sight of its type in the take is.
- **A missile's owner is in its record** (offset 0xE8: unit type and id) and nothing in the repo reads it; `hurt` events list missiles by type and distance only. With it a drop's `near` could name the shooter.
- **mobs.py plays a type as ranged only by the threat table's flag.** These let hostile missiles go in the takes in 40 attack runs or more (an enchantment's sparks among them) and are melee, or nothing, in the arena: Gargoyle Trap (273): 72, Hydra (351): 58, Hydra (352): 58, Hydra (353): 64, Dark Stalker (45): 131, Death Brawler (530): 42, Blood Clan (55): 41, Carver Shaman (59): 103, Dark Shaman (61): 63, Flayer Shaman (662): 40.
- **The threat table's `hard` factor is on every shot of the arena** (Black Soul 640: 5.0). The takes hold 34 Black Soul shots and 0 sole hits: nothing to refit it on.
- **cut.py calls the main set's physical resistance unknown; the manifests have it.** 14 takes whose `character_stats` show the main set in hand (fire 190, lightning 179, cold 189 before the penalty) hold stat 36 (`damageresist`) at 10 in 13 and -90 in one (a curse), the same 10 as the staff's set.
- **A shooter's path record seldom names whom it shoots at**: 0.17 of 4,514 shots with a record named the character, while 0.26 of the missiles ended on it. So `aimed` in a `hurt` event's `by` understates the shooters.
