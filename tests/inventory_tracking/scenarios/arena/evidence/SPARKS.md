## 7. What the takes cannot say

- **Why a release comes.** Neither every hit nor every second of being struck gives one; the best one rate does is the deviance above. Hits under a 128th of the owner's life are not seen.
- **Why a spark that reaches the character takes nothing.** One number stands for a miss, a block, a spark of next to no points and this character's protection; another character would need its own.
- **The size of a spark's blow.** No drop is a spark's in 2,280 sparks.
- **A companion in the way.** A spark that ends near a companion is counted; whether it hurt it is not looked at (nothing in the arena hurts a companion).
