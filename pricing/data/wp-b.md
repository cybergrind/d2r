**Unit:** Ist = 1.0 via `pricing/data/wp-f-ladder.json` (field `ist`, joint NL solution, 2026-09-18): Pul .57 · Um .67 · Mal .79 · Ist 1 · Gul 1.43 · Vex 2.59 · Ohm 4.05 · Lo 5.89 · Sur 8.23 · Cham 8.57 · Ber 9.32 · Jah 11.42 · Zod 13.67. Runes below Pul are not in the WP-F ladder: Lem 0.2 = diablo2.io 2026-08-28 NL band midpoint; sub-Lem runes 0.1 (flagged); PGem 1/40, PAmethyst 1/12 Ist (guide line "Ist ≈ 40 PGems / 12 PAmethyst").
**Price reading (v2):** Traderie `prices[].group` — same group = summed, different groups = OR-alternatives (WP-F verified on ~1,200 multi-group listings); each listing is valued at its cheapest rune/pgem alternative. Listings whose only alternatives are non-rune items are counted, not priced; "offer" = make-offer. *All medians/minima were regenerated with this rule and the WP-F ladder; v1 numbers (alternatives summed, §1.2 midpoints) are superseded — the >30 % movers are listed in `pricing/data/wp-b-v2-changes.txt` (113 stats in 82 white buckets: 87 down, 26 up).*
**Feed/filter:** Traderie JSON API, up to 4 pages (200 listings) per item id, no server filter; client-side 799=softcore, 800=false (NL), 798=PC, 1854 not LoD/classic. 45 items (33 seed + 6 glove bases for the Jav blue + 6 added from WP-A's top-20 once `wp-a-bases.json` landed), 7,778 listings, 4,981 in scope. Buckets = sockets / eth / rarity / ≥15 ED (425 armor, 510 weapon) / res (441, paladin shields) / +bow skills (454); white rows carrying affix or staff-mod props are tagged `affixed` and excluded from "clean base" numbers. **All numbers are asking prices**; thin = n<3. Per-bucket data incl. 3 cheapest asks: `pricing/data/wp-b-prices.json`; regenerate with `python3 pricing/tools/wp_b_buckets.py pricing/data/wp-b-prices.json`.
