## 3 · Rares & crafts — the few patterns worth anything, and why the rest is vendor food

* **The Traderie rare feed is unusable:** 7 pages of Ring (274 listings) and Amulet (333) — **267/268 ring and 304/323 amulet SC/NL/PC listings are one seller** (id 3508427996) asking 1–12 Jah for ordinary 10-FCR rares; the crafted *type* items are similar walls (Blood Ring 100 listings / 1 seller; Caster Amulet 98 / 5; Caster Boots 49 / 4). Recorded as `RR-rare-*-traderie` with the pattern counts, not as prices. **Crafted rarity (797 = crafted) never appears under the Ring/Amulet ids** — crafts live under the 36 crafted-type ids.
* **What the price-check threads say (diablo2.io, fetched 2026-09-18):** rare ring 104 AR / 8 LL / 10 dex / 31 life / 38 cold res → "4–4.5 point ring … cold res is not popular to fighters … Ohm to Lo" vs "first is 4+2 not so wanted … Ist"; a 5–5.5-point all-res/LL/AR ring → "more than a Jah … might end in 2 Jah". Rare sorc amulet +2 / 10 FCR / poison res 37 → "around Jah" corrected to "Ohm – Lo", dissent "I'd rather use a 3/10 blue amulet … the other modifiers rolled poorly", "no value caster … sell to vendor directly". Crafted Warlock caster amulet → "Jah +", "2–3 Jah", "10 Jah if you can wait 3 months". +2 Warlock / 13 res / Teleport-charge amulet → "tele charges save it, otherwise Mara's would be better, ~Ist". +2 Barb / 20 FCR / 1os circlet → "Charsi food … the socket actually takes away an affix since Larzuk can give one on top … blank 2/20s are easy to get".
* **Patterns that are worth listing (each backed by a thread or by build demand):** `RR-caster-amulet` +2 class skills & 10 FCR & (res ≥ 15 or life ≥ 40 or tele charges) → Ist … Jah (thread ranges); `RR-melee-ring` AR ≥ 100 & LL ≥ 5 & (all res ≥ 10 or life ≥ 30) & 10+ str/dex → Ohm … 2 Jah; `RR-caster-ring` 10 FCR & res ≥ 10 & life/mana → Ist–Vex (Traderie: single non-wall listing at Vex); `CR-caster-amulet` 20 FCR (10 craft + 10 suffix) & +2 skills → Jah+ (thread), Warlock skills currently the hot version; `CR-blood-ring` LL + str + life + res: named by 8 builds but no priced non-wall listing — thin. Circlets: 2/20 needs FRW/IAS/sockets on top, else Charsi.
* **Why most rares are vendor food** (thread reasoning + affix math): a rare needs 4–6 *matching* affixes (e.g. caster: FCR + skills + res + life; melee: AR + LL + res + stats) and each slot has 1–2 "must" affixes (10 FCR on rings/amulets, +2 skills on amulets/circlets, 20 FCR on circlets); one wrong affix ("energy instead of something else", "cold res is not popular to fighters", "poison res is good but not good enough on its own") drops it to Ist or Charsi. Crafts beat rares only because the recipe *guarantees* the must-affixes (Caster amulet: +5–10 FCR, mana, regen; Blood ring: 1–3 % LL, +1–5 str, +10–20 life; Blood gloves: LL, 5–10 % CB, life — maxroll crafted-items 2026-03-01, 100 % four affixes at ilvl 71+) and the rest still has to roll.
