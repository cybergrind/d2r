# WP-G · White bases — which are worth a lot, and why (SC / Non-Ladder / PC / RotW, 2026-09-18)

**Unit:** Ist = 1.0 (WP-F ladder, 2026-09-18: Pul .57 · Um .67 · Mal .79 · Gul 1.43 · Vex 2.59 · Ohm 4.05 · Lo 5.89 · Sur 8.23 · Cham 8.57 · Ber 9.32 · Jah 11.4 · Zod 13.7). **Asks** = Traderie asking prices from WP-B (n = listings in the bucket; WP-B shows 3–30× ask inflation on chase rolls vs the diablo2.io guide of 2026-08-28 — quote the *min* for floor items, expect fills below the median). **Fills** = diablo2.io trades marked sold ≤3 days (`irarity=4 activesold=1 hc=2 ladder=2 plat_pc=1 legacy_resu=2`, 18 spaced searches, raw `pricing/raw/d2io/search-wpg-*.html`). Demand weight = WP-A (S=2/A=1 over 26 builds). JSON twin: `pricing/data/wp-g-bases.json` (keys `BS-…`); rebuild with `python3 pricing/tools/wp_g_build.py`.

**One-line answer.** A white base is worth runes only when *three* things coincide: (1) an S/A-tier runeword wants exactly that base (demand), (2) the drop already has the *exact* socket count that Larzuk/cube cannot manufacture (supply), and (3) the roll multiplies it — superior 15 % ED, ethereal on a mercenary weapon/armor, or a 45-all-res / +3-skill automod. Miss any one and the item is Pul–Mal "floor", however high its demand weight. Ranking = median ask of the best clean-white bucket (n≥3 priced) in WP-B.

## How value multiplies (asks, WP-B 2026-09-18)

Same base, four rolls: **Giant Thresher** eth 6os normal 0.2→1.0 (n=16) → eth **4os** normal 5.9→11.4 (n=20) → eth 4os **superior** 57→86 (n=10) → eth 4os superior **15 ED** 126→799 (n=8, 4 priced). **Archon Plate** 4os normal 0.08→1.0 (n=23) → 4os superior 15ED 11.4→20.7 (n=4) → **3os** superior 15ED 22.8→45.7 (n=5) → 3os eth superior 15ED "40× Jah" (n=2, unpriced fantasy). **Sacred Targe** 4os <40@ floor → 4os 45@ 9.3→17.3 (n=14) → 4os 45@ sup 15ED 57→126 (n=7) → eth 45@ 45.7→63 (n=9). **Berserker Axe** 5os normal 0.79→1.0 (n=13) → 5os sup 15ED 34→45.7 (n=10) → eth 6os sup 15ED 45.7→51 (n=4). Each step is a *different scarcity*, so they multiply rather than add:

* **Superior** = 5–15 % Enhanced Defense / Enhanced Damage, and/or 10–15 % max durability, +1–3 AR (base-items, May 12, 2024); 15 is the chase roll. Superior bases "can be Ethereal and spawn with Sockets" but **cannot be cube-socketed** (sockets page, June 16, 2026) — so a superior base with the right socket count on drop is rarer than a normal one, and a superior 0os base can only get Larzuk's *maximum*.
* **Ethereal** = +50 % Defense or base Weapon Damage, cannot be repaired, reduced durability ((max/2)+1); Zod socket or an Indestructible runeword (BotD, Void) removes the drawback (durability page, Feb 10, 2026). "Ethereal Items do not suffer from Durability loss when equipped on a Mercenary" (mercenary-mechanics, Feb 10, 2026) — hence eth polearms/Sacred Armor for the merc, non-eth ("Superior …") bases for the character, and eth being *irrelevant* on the Indestructible Phase Blade.
* **Sockets** = "Runes must be placed in a specific Base Item type with the EXACT amount of Sockets" (runewords page, June 2, 2026). Max sockets depend on ilvl (table ilvl 1-25 / 26-40 / 41+; Normal difficulty caps at 3, Nightmare 4, Hell uncapped; 1/3 of eligible drops are socketed at all — sockets page). A drop's socket count is fixed unless it is 0.
* **Automods** (Sacred Targe 5–45 all res *or* ED/AR; Amazon bows +1–3 skills) are a fourth axis on those two classes (base-items).
* **Weapon speed** is a base property, not a roll: Weapon Speed Modifier ranges 20…−60, negative = faster and "adds directly to your Anim Rate" (attack-speed, Feb 10, 2026). Phase Blade −30, Mancatcher −20, Thresher/Matriarchal Bow/Flail −10, Berserker Axe/GT/GPA/CV/Crystal Sword 0, Colossus Blade +5, Cryptic Axe/GMB +10 (base-items).

Corollary: for bases whose ilvl-41+ maximum **equals** the runeword's socket count, every drop is a base and the white market is floor — Monarch 4 (Spirit), Mage Plate 3 (Treachery/Enigma), Dusk Shroud/Archon Plate 4 (CoH/Fortitude), Colossus Voulge 4 (Insight), Bone Shield 2 (Rhyme). Where the maximum **exceeds** it — Giant Thresher/Great Poleaxe 6 vs Infinity 4, Thresher/Cryptic Axe/Mancatcher 5 vs 4, Berserker Axe 6 vs Beast 5, Archon Plate 4 vs Enigma 3, Grand Matron Bow 5 vs Faith 4, Phase Blade 6 vs Grief 5 — the exact-count drop carries the premium.

## Why the floor is the floor (supply vs demand)

* **Plain 4os Monarch (0.42→0.79, n=19), 4os/3os Dusk Shroud (0.57→0.79), 3os Mage Plate (0.2→0.73, n=31), 4os Archon Plate (0.08→1.0, n=23):** Larzuk turns every such drop into the required socket count, so supply = every Hell drop of the base; buyers who spend Jah + Ber (≈21 Ist) on Enigma runes pay for the 15 ED superior roll, not the plain base. Demand weight 39–41 does not help a plain base because the *runes*, not the base, are the cost.
* **Crystal Sword, Flail (all white buckets 0.08→1.0), Diadem:** high demand, floor market — the sockets page names Crystal Sword and Flail among the "very common items" from Normal Cow Level (alvl 28) that "receive 4 Sockets from Larzuk"; both are normal-tier (qlvl 11 / 19) and drop from Act 1 onward; Spirit, CtA, HotO are caster runewords whose base damage is irrelevant ("Ethereal for style"). Diadem's demand is shared with "Any Barbarian Helmet or Druid Pelt with +x to Skills" (runewords page) and the white supply is negligible (Magic Level 18; 196/200 listings magic/rare), so there is no market either way — its value sits in the rune (Dream = Jah, Flickering Flame = Vex).
* **Non-ethereal mercenary bases** (non-eth 4os Thresher/GT/CV/SA 0.2→1.0): no merc build wants them, no character can use a 152–232 str two-hander well — demand ≈ 0.
* **The one 45@/+3 automod exception:** Sacred Targe and Grand Matron Bow have no floor at 4os *if* the automod rolled max — the automod is drop luck Larzuk cannot supply.
