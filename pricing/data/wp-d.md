# WP-D · Pickup filter for Pindleskin runs — 2026-09-18 (SC / NL / PC / RotW)

**Drop ilvl, derived once.** maxroll *Sockets* (Last Updated June 16, 2026): "An item's ilvl is determined by the monster that drops it, or the Area Level (alvl) for chests and other clickables", with the worked example "The Cow King and his Minions are Monster Level (mlvl) 31 … Any item dropped in this area has ilvl 28/31" — i.e. monster drops carry ilvl = mlvl. maxroll *Pindleskin Farming Guide* (Feb 10, 2026): "a Monster Level of 86, both he and his minions". ⇒ **Pindle's pack drops ilvl 86 ⇒ read the "ilvl 41+" column of the socket table; "Hell Difficulty does not have a Socket limit."** Other mechanics used: 1/3 of bases spawn socketed; a socketed base "shows up in a Gray color; just like an Ethereal. You need to pick up the item to see the difference"; cube socket recipes need an *unsocketed* Normal/Ethereal base (not Superior/Low Quality) and roll by the base's max (max 4 → 4os 50 %; max 5 → 5os 33 %; max 6 → each 16.67 %); Larzuk gives bases "their maximum possible number of Sockets which depend on their Item Levels" (magic 1–2 random, rare/unique 1); Pindle "always drops 2 items of Magic quality or higher (unless … a Rune or Gem)", MF changes neither rune drops nor base tier. Prices = WP-B Traderie **asks** (2026-09-18) in Ist via the WP-F ladder, n = listings in bucket. Full evidence per rule: `wp-d-pickup.json`.

## Verify in-game (not asserted)
1. **Minion ilvl = 86** rests on the Cow King example (mlvl → ilvl) plus the guide's "both he and his minions" mlvl 86; a native 4os Monarch or 6os Phase Blade from the pack confirms ilvl ≥ 41. The plan's snippet that Nihlathak's Temple "may become terrorized" (RotW) would change mlvl/ilvl — and would re-open 41–45-life GCs.
2. **Larzuk on an already-socketed base**: the sockets page only says bases get their max; whether he takes a socketed item is not stated (rules assume no). Larzuk quests per difficulty: not on the page.
3. Sockets page inconsistency: Larzuk example "Any Monarch always receives 4 Sockets" vs table Monarch 3 at ilvl ≤ 40.
4. **Grimoire sockets** (max 2) come only from the diablo2.io base page (~7 months old); grimoires are absent from the maxroll socket table. No plain "Mask" row either (Death Mask 2\|2\|3) — Wisdom "Mask" rule is by analogy.
5. **Staff-mods on white claws/daggers**: Traderie tags 3os "normal/affixed" claw rows with LS/MB/DF; purediablo (WP-C) says staff mods never appear on normal claws. Decide by looking at a dropped white claw.
6. **Gloves ceiling**: WP-B saw only +2 Jav/20 IAS rares; d2io guide says +3. Check an ID'd pair.
7. Eth Sacred Targe 45@ asks (Ber+) — demand source unverified (asks only, no fills).
8. Whether Pindle's guaranteed "2 items of Magic quality or higher" also applies to his minions (page says "he"). The rules assume whites come from minions/extra drops.
9. All prices are asks; WP-B had no fills for these buckets (diablo2.io slot was reserved). Spot-check 3os 15ED Archon, 4os 15ED Monarch, eth 4os GT/GPA with `activesold=1` before quoting Jah-tier numbers.
