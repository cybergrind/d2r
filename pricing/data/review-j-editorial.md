## Verdict

The primer is complete as an evidence file and honest about its evidence — every number has a feed, n and date, the ledger is real, the mechanics "whys" (Larzuk maxima, exact-socket gate, facet stacking, +life alvl gate, craft guaranteed affixes) genuinely explain prices and will survive stale numbers. It fails as a decision tool: the TL;DR prices but does not decide, the verdict words are undefined and "keep" is used for the highest sale band, the quoting rule lives in a collapsed block and is contradicted by the ledger, the §3/§4 headline medians are ceilings the rows themselves disown (trash rows "worth" 11 Ist), the D-2 "0os" shortcut would have a reader destroy the top-ranked base, and the flow's fallbacks point at WP-A lists that are not in the file, leaving magic items, large charms, off-list whites, non-jewelry rares and most sets without a rule. Fix findings 1–8 and 11–13 (roughly one day of edits, no new pulls), add a realistic-band column and the missing branches, cut §5.1 and the duplicated rules, add anchors and a stat glossary — then a second pass with the fact-check lens can be worth running.
