## RotW items

**Sunder charms** (maxroll Sunder Charms 2026-05-22; new-items 2026-02-19): immune → 95 % res for the holder only; self-res penalty −70…−90 (magic −45…−65, physical +10–20 % dmg taken). *Latent X + Perfect gem + rune + Worldstone Shard(s) → Renewed X* with up to 5 affixes, one per group: element (5–15 % skill dmg **or** −5–10 % enemy res; Bone Break +75–100 % ED or −5–10 % phys res), MF 14–25 / GF 20–55, life 10–65 / mana 10–75, FRW 5–10 / FHR 12–24 / +3–8 attrs, MDR 5–10 / DR 5–10. Observed on every Renewed row: self-res shows −70 (not on the page — observed). Asks (min · median): **base** fire/poison 0.2 · 1, cold 0.57 · 1, lightning 0.67 · 1, magic 0.79 · 9.3, physical 0.57 · 9.3 (fill: Bone Break 11 % → **1 Ist**). **Latent** poison 0.4 · 0.79, cold 1 · 2.6, fire 2.6 · 4.05 (fill −89 → **1 Ist**), lightning 9.3 · 11.4, magic 9.3 · 11.4, physical 18.6 · 22.8 (d2io swap ads: "my Latent Fire/Poison for your Latent Lightning/Magic/Physical"). **Renewed** cold 1 · 11.4, fire 0.67 · 22.8, lightning 1 · 17, poison 0.2 · 2.6, magic 4 · 91, physical 11.4 · 74; fills: Renewed Flame Rift −10/24 FHR/49 mana/24 MF → **15 Jah (171)**; Renewed Black Cleft −10/+8 attr/43 life/DR 7/25 MF → **35 Jah (400)**; perfect Renewed Cold Rupture 15 % csd/25 MF/+7 attr → **6 Jah**; Renewed Flame Rift −10/24 FHR/39 life → **5 Jah**; mid rolls → 1 Ber, 1 Ber, 1 Lo, 1 Vex; low → Pul+Lem, 25 PGems, 2 P.Amethyst. Why the order: magic/physical immunities are otherwise unbreakable ("Magic Resistance cannot be reduced in any way", Immunities 2026-02-10), lightning is the most-played S-tier element, and −enemy-res on the charm works at 100 % on sundered mobs while Conviction/Lower Resist drop to 1/5.

**Colossal Ancient Jewels** (new-items + Colossal Ancients pages, 2026-02-19): Defender's Fire / Defender's Bile (Talic), Protector's Frost / Protector's Stone (Korlic), Guardian's Thunder / Guardian's Light (Madawc); lvl 75; −5–10 % enemy res + 5–10 % skill dmg (Stone: +30–50 % ED, −phys res) + 3–5 % exp + 25–50 % GF + 15–35 % MF; "strictly better versions than their Facet counterparts", **1 per character**. They trade: Traderie lists all six by these exact names (no catalog hit for "colossal"/"ancient jewel" — that is why the plan found nothing). Asks (in scope n · min · median Ist): Fire 70 · 1.0 · 5.9; Bile 54 · 0.67 · 1.9; Frost 32 · 9.3 · 22.8 (only 3 priced in runes, rest "offer"; 10/10/45 GF/32 MF → 8 Jah); Stone 72 · 0.79 · 5.9; Thunder 59 · 1.8 · 8.2; Light 76 · 0.4 · 11.4; top rolls (−10 or MF ≥ 30) median 20–108. diablo2.io: one sold row pays with one ("Latent Bone Break → 1 Guardian's Thunder"; seller "looking for a Guardian's Thunder −8-10 %"). Read: −5/15 MF ≈ a 5/5 facet (Ist); −10/35 MF = BiS for the element's S-tier builds (multi-Jah).
