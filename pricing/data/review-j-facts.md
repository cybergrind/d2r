## Verdict

The primer's skeleton is sound: the currency ladder, the white-base buckets, the jewel/charm buckets, the misc per-unit table and the mechanics quotes are copied faithfully from the hand-offs and the hand-offs match the raw pulls. The errors cluster in three places. First, §5 inherits two systematic WP-I defects — bands that are really min–q1 while medians sit 3–10× higher (HoZ, Verdungo's, CoA, Andariel's, Nightwing's…), and item-page fills truncated from multi-item lots to a single rune — so the uniques section overstates fills and understates asks in opposite directions and should not be acted on until WP-I is rebuilt. Second, a handful of headline ratios are arithmetic slips (fills "45–70 %" of asks is 32–63 %; "87–98 %" RotW-tagged is 71–98 %; Jah Ladder band; facet and Gheed's multiples), and one bucket in the TL;DR (Archon 3os sup 15ED n=5) is a silent merge with mislabelled rows. Third, quotation marks are used loosely: about a dozen "quotes" from fills, threads and maxroll are paraphrases, and two ledger items ("fat", Hustle) and two date hedges are already answered by cached pages. Nothing found reopens a Rejected idea or a review-log correction (OR rule, per-stack rule, Colossal names, walls all hold); the §7 refresh path is the only claim that would fail outright as written (the solver's output shape does not match the hand-off the other tools read).
