# Deadly packs — plan (2026-10-06)

Goal (user, 2026-10-06): some monster combinations kill by burst — dark archers under a nasty
aura, more so in a dense group. Mark those packs apart from ordinary uniques and champions, early
enough to approach with care. Today every pack leader is the same magenta dot and ground mark
(`ZoneTracker.map_dots`, kind `leader`); a pack of plain archers standing in another pack's
Fanaticism gets no mark at all.

Red/green TDD for every step (development.md). No game input; reads only.

## What is already known (probe logs, 50 runs, 95,316 first sightings, analysed 2026-10-06)

- **Every modifier of a monster is readable**: monster data +0x20.. holds up to 9 monumod ids
  (the tracker reads only the first, for the Terror Zone vote). Uniques show 3 in Hell, e.g.
  `[17, 30, 26]` lightning + aura + teleport. Seen on uniques (flag 0x08, 4,885 sightings): extra
  strong 5 ×1398, extra fast 6 ×1388, cursed 7 ×982, fire 9 ×1424, lightning 17 ×939, cold 18
  ×951, mana burn 25 ×998, teleport 26 ×966, spectral 27 ×1395, stone skin 28 ×982, multishot 29
  ×385, **aura 30 ×1223**. Champions (0x0c): plain 16 ×2706, fanatic 37 ×137, possessed 38 ×143,
  berserker 39 ×151; ghostly 36 ×125 (0x4c).
- **Minions carry their own modifier bytes** (two ids, e.g. `(17, 28)`, `(6, 7)`): what the
  leader passed on is read from the minion, not guessed.
- **The aura itself is readable: stats 350 (skill id) and 351 (skill level)**. All 1,223
  aura-enchanted uniques carry them; 3% of other leaders do. Values on the aura owners: 123 ×~190
  (Conviction), 98 ×~170 (Might), 108 ×~150 (Blessed Aim), 122 ×~140 (Fanaticism), and
  365 / 368 / 369 ×~150 each — not Paladin ids, presumably the monster Holy Fire / Freeze / Shock;
  **unresolved** (R1).
- **Monsters standing in an aura carry the same two stats**: minions (0x10) and plain monsters
  (0x00) show 350 = 108 / 98 / 122 at first sight (628 + 607 + 451 minions, 346 + 268 + 134 plain).
  So "this archer is under Fanaticism" is a fact per monster, not an inference from distance.
  Conviction never shows on monsters (it lands on the player). Limit: stats are read on first
  sight only, so a monster that walks into an aura later is missed (R3).
- **No leader pointer in the first 0x80 bytes of a minion's data** (4,000 minions checked against
  the leaders' unit ids): packs have to be grouped by position (or R4).
- Monsters are streamed from ~50–85 world units (10–17 tiles, about one screen): the warning
  cannot come earlier than that; ranged packs shoot from about that far.
- d2data `monstats.json` (pricing/raw/d2data) gives per type: `rangedtype`, missiles
  (`MissA1`…), Hell damage (`A1MaxD(H)`, `El1MaxD(H)`/`El1Pct(H)`/`El1Type`), skills, group size
  (`MinGrp`/`MaxGrp`, `PartyMin`/`PartyMax`). 187 killable rows have a missile or `rangedtype`,
  in 37 families. Whether those numbers are the RotW ones is unchecked (R1).

| Class | Families (d2data BaseId) | Why |

**R3 — conditions (multipliers).** For each, where it is read and what it multiplies:

| Condition | Read from | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| aura skill + level | stats 350/351 on the monster | Fanaticism, Might, Blessed Aim: physical; Conviction and the three elemental auras: the owner marks its surroundings |
| unique modifiers | data +0x20.. on the leader, inherited ones on each minion | Extra Strong, Extra Fast, Cursed, Multishot, Lightning/Fire/Cold Enchanted, Mana Burn |

Open in R3: (a) re-reading stats 350/351 for present monsters every few passes (cost: one stat
list per monster; the probe reads them once today) versus spreading the aura by distance from
its owner; (b) the aura radius per skill level; (c) whether the Conviction owner's reach can be

**R4 — ground truth without asking the user.** The probe logs have no player life. Add it (stat
6/7 of the player unit, already read for the level) to the probe's summary, at every pass while
it drops. Then a script over the logs lists burst events — life lost ≥ 30% within 1 s, and
deaths — with the monsters present (type, modifiers, aura, count, distance). That list both
checks the R2/R3 weights and finds combinations the tables miss. The weights are tuned until
the events sit in the top band and ordinary packs do not; no labelling by the user.

## Model (`terror/danger.py`, pure: no memory reads, no GTK)

- `Threat` per live monster: family weight (R2) × its own multipliers (R3: modifiers, aura stat,
  zone modifier, level). Monsters of no listed family score 0 unless a melee-burst rule applies.
- `Pack`: live hostile monsters clustered by distance (single link, ~6 tiles; R4 may give a
  better key). Pack score = sum of member threats, i.e. density is the sum itself, then × area
  multipliers (a Conviction or curser pack within a screen; a curse on the player).
- With the character's stats (later phase) the score is expected damage per second as a share
  of life; until then it is unitless and the bands come from R4.
- Two bands: `caution` and `deadly`. Config: thresholds, radius, on/off
  (`APPRAISAL.danger_marks`, `HudGroundConfig.kinds`).
- The tracker keeps per unit what the model needs: txt id, modifier bytes, aura stat — all from
  the `seen` event already logged, so every existing probe log replays through the model
  (tests use real excerpts, like the Black Marsh replay).

## Highlight

1. **Map card**: new dot kinds `danger` (deadly) and `caution`, every member of the pack, not
   only the leader: a colour not used yet (leaders are magenta, mobs toned-down red, Heralds
   their own): saturated orange-red for deadly, amber for caution, larger than `mob` (3 px), no
   rings (user, 2026-10-03). Drawn above leaders, below POIs.
2. **Ground marks**: a filled red ellipse under every member of a deadly pack (leaders keep
   theirs; a deadly leader takes the danger colour), following live positions — the members'
   path addresses go into the payload like leaders' do (`hud/live.py`). Out of view: one arrow
   per pack (its centre), not per member, with no 15-tile cut-off (`arrow_range`), so the
   warning shows as soon as the pack is streamed.
3. **Warning line**: on the Terror card slot (or its own slot when no Terror card), one row per
   deadly pack, nearest first, with the reason: `↗  ⚠ Dark Ranger x9 · Fanaticism · Cursed: north-east`.
   The reason is what makes the mark trusted, and it is the check against R4.
4. Not planned: sound, screen flash. Say so if wanted.

## Phases

1. R1 extract + `threats.json` build script and tests.
2. Tracker keeps modifiers and aura stat per unit (from `seen`); replay test over a real log.
3. R4: player life in the probe; burst-event script; first run over new logs.
4. R2/R3 tables with weights; `danger.py` model + tests (aura archers > plain archers > plain
   melee; density adds; zone modifier raises; allies and dead never count).
5. Map dots, then ground marks and the pack arrow, then the warning line.
6. Tune bands from R4 events; then the character's life/resistances in the score.
7. Later: aura re-read (R3a), curse on the player, melee-burst rules, Nightmare.

## Status (2026-10-06)

Done, with tests (tests/inventory_tracking/terror, hud, osd):

- **R1**: the install's `monstats.txt`, `skills.txt`, `monumod.txt` and monster names are read by
  `terror/build_threats.py` into `terror/data/threats.json` (627 monster types). Aura ids
  resolved: 365 `MonHolyFreeze`, 368 `MonHolyFire`, 369 `MonHolyShock`; 98 Might, 108 Blessed
  Aim, 122 Fanaticism, 123 Conviction. Stats 350/351 are `modifierlist_skill` /
  `modifierlist_level` (itemstatcost.txt). Aura range = `Param1 + (level - 1) x Param2` world
  units (Might 16 + 2/level, Fanaticism 11 + 1, Conviction 20 flat, the three Holy auras 6 + 1).
  d2data's monstats.json equals the game's for the damage, level, group and skill columns.
- **R2**: a ranged monster's numbers are those of the attack that fires its missile (`MissA1`
  -> A1, `MissA2` -> A2, `MissC` -> the `SC` element): Tainted 2.0 lightning, Gloams 1.9
  lightning, skeleton mages 1.5–2.0, Dark Rangers 0.9 physical (0.6–1.2 by variant), slingers
  0.85, Serpent Magi 0.6 + 1.0 magic. Casters whose damage is in a skill (Oblivion and Abyss
  Knights, vampires, succubi, council members, Doom Casters, Unravelers) have no number in
  monstats: their melee swing stands in, as magic — the weakest part of the table. Elemental
  damage counts at 25% (resistances at the Hell cap), so physical shooters outrank elemental
  ones until Conviction (x3).
- **Model** (`terror/danger.py`): threat x own modifiers x auras (own stat or an owner in
  range) x curses nearby; melee at 0.35 and at most 8 per pack; packs by single link at 6 tiles.
  Bands: caution 9, deadly 12. Replay of the 50 logs (first-sight positions, top three packs
  every 3 s): 16% of those packs reach 9, 9.6% reach 12. Most frequent deadly labels there:
  `Burning Dead x15 · Extra Strong · Cursed · Blessed Aim`, `Hell Bovine x21 · Extra Strong ·
  Cursed · Extra Fast`. Eight Dark Rangers under Fanaticism score 12.6.
- **Tracker**: keeps txt id, all modifier bytes and the aura per unit from the `seen` event;
  `ZoneTracker.packs`, `map_dots` (kinds `danger`, `caution`, `pack`), `danger_lines`.
- **Highlight**: map dots orange (`danger`, 3.5 px) and amber (`caution`, 3 px) for every member;
  filled ground marks under a deadly pack's monsters, followed live; one arrow per deadly pack
  to its centre within 40 tiles; warning row at the top of the Terror card slot, outside Terror
  Zones too. `APPRAISAL.danger_marks` turns all of it off.
- **Held marks** (2026-10-06, user: the marks toggled): the bands were plain thresholds on a
  score that changes every pass (kills, a step out of an owner's aura, packs merging and
  splitting). A pack most of which was marked on the last pass now keeps its band down to
  `release` (0.75) of the band's score. Replay of the 12 newest logs at 0.5 s steps, marks
  that flipped back within 5 s: 1461 → 677 with first-sight positions, 7354 → 3834 with a
  simulated walk. Real movement is not in the logs, so the rest is unmeasured.
- **R4, first half**: the probe logs the player's life whenever it changed (`life` events), and
  `terror/bursts.py` lists the bursts (30% of life within a second, deaths) with the packs
  nearby and their bands. No log has `life` events yet.
