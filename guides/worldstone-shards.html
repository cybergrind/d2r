Worldstone Shards — where and how to farm each one

Diablo II: Resurrected, Softcore · Non-Ladder · PC · Reign of the Warlock. Drop tables read from the installed game on 2026-10-05, the mix by area level recomputed 2026-10-07. Rates are computed from those tables, not measured in play; the in-game tracker (§6) is collecting the measurements.

Kill Hell elites in an area of the right level, outside terror zones. Only champions, unique pack bosses, super uniques and Heralds drop shards; white monsters and act bosses never do. Outside a terror zone the shard is weighted by the elite's monster level, not by the act: area level 67–71 favours Deep, 72–75 Northern (Catacombs), 76–78 Eastern, 79 and up Western (most of Acts 3–5 and every level-85 area). No area favours Southern. In a terror zone every shard is equally likely (20% each). The per-elite shard chance is the same in and out of terror zones.

Preconditions & working assumptions
  • Hell difficulty, players 1. Elite and Herald tables have NoDrop 0, so player count does not change the shard chance per kill.
  • Negative Picks are read the classic way: take the listed entries in order, each Prob times, up to |Picks|. The shard parent classes (Act N Worldstone Shard Parent …, Picks −2) therefore evaluate the act-weighted table and the even table once each; the ConditionCalc on those two tables lets exactly one of them pay out.
  • The condition cond('Desecrated') is taken to mean "the area is currently terrorized".
  • Treasure class upgrade (added 2026-10-07). In Hell an elite does not drop from its monster's own class but from the last class of the same group whose level its monster level reaches; a class is never downgraded. Monster level is the area's MonLvlEx(H) (the monster's own level for boss monsters), +2 for a champion, +3 for a unique or super unique. This is the classic game's rule, assumed to hold in this build; it matches the Catacombs observation in the review log and is what the tracker in §6 checks.
  • An area's shard mix is the plain average over the monster types in its Hell spawn list (nmon1–25). After the upgrade nearly every area has one mix for all its monsters, so spawn weights hardly matter.
  • "Elite packs" is MonUMin(H)–MonUMax(H) from levels.txt. It counts champion and unique packs together.
  • In-game locations of named super uniques are general game knowledge, not read from the tables.
Rejected ideas (and why)
  • "Farm the act the shard terrorizes." Wrong by the tables: the shard tables are shifted against the acts, and the class an elite drops from follows its level, not its act (§2).
  • "The mix follows the monster's home act" (this guide until 2026-10-07). It ignored the treasure class upgrade. Almost every Hell elite is upgraded past its own act's class, so Act 3, Act 4 and the cows give Western, not Northern or Eastern.
  • "Terror zones drop more shards per elite." The terrorized elite classes (… Desecrated, Picks −2) hold one entry, the ordinary class, so the roll is identical. Before RotW the second entry was the Sunder Charm roll.
  • Act bosses, Council, Countess, Nihlathak, Summoner, Radament, Izual, Hephasto for shards. Their own classes have no shard entry.
  • Clearing white monsters for shards. Regular classes (Act N (H) H2H/Cast/Miss …) have no shard entry. White kills only matter in terror zones, where they advance Herald spawns.
  • Quoting d2data alone. Its terror-zone config is known stale (see the terror zones guide), so every number here was recomputed from the installed game.

1 · How shards drop

Shards are an entry inside the ordinary Hell elite item tables. Each time an elite's item roll lands on that entry you get exactly one shard.

  • Hell only. The shard tables carry the condition cond('Difficulty', hell).
  • Quest gate. They also carry QuestFlagEx 40; Maxroll states shards need the Hell Baal quest completed.
  • Magic Find and player count do nothing. A shard is not a quality roll, and the elite tables have no "no drop" entry to shrink.
  • Terrorized or not, same chance. Only the mix changes (§2).

2 · Which shard you get

Outside a terror zone an elite rolls one of five shard tables. Which one depends on the class it drops from, and in Hell that class is upgraded by monster level: area level +2 for a champion, +3 for a unique pack boss or super unique. A monster never drops below its own act's class, so Act 5 monsters are always on the Western table.

4 · Terror zones and Heralds

  • Same chance per elite, even mix. A terrorized elite gives each shard type at 0.45–0.48% per kill (0.96% for a super unique), about half the targeted rate in §2.
  • Heralds are the terror-zone bonus. Each Herald kill gives 7.4% (tier 1–2), 11.1% (tier 3–4) or 14.7% (tier 5) for a shard, always the even mix: 1.5% / 2.2% / 2.9% for any one type. Routes for spawning them are in the terror zones guide.
  • Use terror zones when you want all five shards or are farming Heralds anyway. Use §3 when you want one specific shard.
  • Avoid your target's best area while it is terrorized. Terrorized Catacombs drop Northern at 20% instead of 42%.
  • Southern is the exception: terror zones (20%) beat every ordinary area (15%).

5 · How to run it

  1. Play Hell, players 1. Higher player counts make elites tougher without raising the shard chance.
  2. Skip white monsters and act bosses. Teleport pack to pack; kill every champion in a champion pack and the boss of a unique pack.
  3. Chain waypoint areas, then remake. For example Catacombs 2 → 3 → 4 for Northern, Cold Plains → Stony Field → Dark Wood → Black Marsh for Deep, Dry Hills → Far Oasis → Lost City for Eastern, or Frigid Highlands (Eldritch) → Anya's portal (Pindleskin) for Western.
  4. Prefer super uniques at fixed spots. They are worth two elite rolls and need no searching.
  5. Check the terror-zone message first. If your target area is terrorized, farm a different shard or go Herald hunting instead.

Build. Maxroll's elite-hunting tier list (2026-05-22) ranks Berserk Barbarian S, the current Echoing Strike Warlock A, and Summoner Warlock B. Echoing Strike needs no change for this. Summoner Warlock (bound Dark Lancer with Cursed and Fanaticism, Goatman, Defiler, Enigma) is the hands-off option at lower speed.

Verify in-game (not asserted anywhere above as measured)

  • The class upgrade by monster level (§2). One observation so far: the user's Hell Catacombs runs gave mostly Northern shards (2026-10-07, not counted). The band edges (71/72, 75/76, 78/79) are the least certain part.
  • That one shard drops per shard-entry hit (the negative-Picks reading in the assumptions).
  • Unique-pack minions: assumed to use the regular table and so drop no shards.
  • Whether the Barbarian's Find Item can produce shards from an elite corpse. The tables do not exclude it; the engine check is unknown.
  • Actual shards per hour for any route. No timings were measured.

The tracker

From 2026-10-07 serve logs every elite kill (kind, area, terrorized or not) and every Worldstone Shard first seen on the ground to terror-probe.jsonl. This command sets the shards seen against what the model above expects, per act and split into regular, terror and unsure (terror state not known):

uv run --offline python -m inventory_tracking.terror.shards [--levels]

The model lives in inventory_tracking/terror/shards.py; its table is rebuilt from the install with python -m inventory_tracking.terror.build_shards <install>.
