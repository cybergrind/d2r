Worldstone Shards — where and how to farm each one

Diablo II: Resurrected, Softcore · Non-Ladder · PC · Reign of the Warlock. Drop tables read from the installed game on 2026-10-05. Rates are computed from those tables, not measured in play.

Kill Hell elites in the right act, outside terror zones. Only champions, unique pack bosses, super uniques and Heralds drop shards; white monsters and act bosses never do. Outside a terror zone the shard you get is weighted by the monster's home act, and the weighting is shifted: Act 5 monsters favour Western, Act 4 Eastern, Act 1 Southern, Act 2 Deep, Act 3 Northern. In a terror zone every shard is equally likely (20% each), so terror zones are the wrong place to target one specific shard. The per-elite shard chance is the same in and out of terror zones.

Preconditions & working assumptions
  • Hell difficulty, players 1. Elite and Herald tables have NoDrop 0, so player count does not change the shard chance per kill.
  • Negative Picks are read the classic way: take the listed entries in order, each Prob times, up to |Picks|. The shard parent classes (Act N Worldstone Shard Parent …, Picks −2) therefore evaluate the act-weighted table and the even table once each; the ConditionCalc on those two tables lets exactly one of them pay out.
  • The condition cond('Desecrated') is taken to mean "the area is currently terrorized".
  • An area's shard mix is the plain average over the monster types in its Hell spawn list (nmon1–25). Real spawn weights per game are not modelled; this only matters in the few areas that mix acts.
  • "Elite packs" is MonUMin(H)–MonUMax(H) from levels.txt. It counts champion and unique packs together.
  • In-game locations of named super uniques are general game knowledge, not read from the tables.
Rejected ideas (and why)
  • "Farm the act the shard terrorizes." Wrong by the tables: the act-weighted table each act's elites use is shifted (Act 1 elites use the "Act 3" parent, Act 2 the "Act 4", Act 3 the "Act 5", Act 4 the "Act 2", Act 5 the "Act 1"). Act 5 elites drop Northern only 8% of the time.
  • "Terror zones drop more shards per elite." The terrorized elite classes (… Desecrated, Picks −2) hold one entry, the ordinary class, so the roll is identical. Before RotW the second entry was the Sunder Charm roll.
  • Act bosses, Council, Countess, Nihlathak, Summoner, Radament, Izual, Hephasto for shards. Their own classes have no shard entry.
  • Clearing white monsters for shards. Regular classes (Act N (H) H2H/Cast/Miss …) have no shard entry. White kills only matter in terror zones, where they advance Herald spawns.
  • Quoting d2data alone. Its terror-zone config is known stale (see the terror zones guide), so every number here was recomputed from the installed game.

1 · How shards drop

Shards are an entry inside the ordinary Hell elite item tables. Each time an elite's item roll lands on that entry you get exactly one shard.

  • Hell only. The shard tables carry the condition cond('Difficulty', hell).
  • Quest gate. They also carry QuestFlagEx 40; Maxroll states shards need the Hell Baal quest completed.
  • Magic Find and player count do nothing. A shard is not a quality roll, and the elite tables have no "no drop" entry to shrink.
  • Terrorized or not, same chance. Only the mix changes (§2).

2 · Which shard you get

Outside a terror zone the mix depends on which act's tables the monster uses, which is almost always the act you are standing in. The Hell cows are the exception: they use Act 4 tables.

4 · Terror zones and Heralds

  • Same chance per elite, even mix. A terrorized elite gives each shard type at 0.45–0.48% per kill (0.96% for a super unique), about half the targeted rate in §2.
  • Heralds are the terror-zone bonus. Each Herald kill gives 7.4% (tier 1–2), 11.1% (tier 3–4) or 14.7% (tier 5) for a shard, always the even mix: 1.5% / 2.2% / 2.9% for any one type. Routes for spawning them are in the terror zones guide.
  • Use terror zones when you want all five shards or are farming Heralds anyway. Use §3 when you want one specific shard.
  • Avoid your target's best act while it is terrorized. Act 3 terrorized drops Northern at 20% instead of 42%.

5 · How to run it

  1. Play Hell, players 1. Higher player counts make elites tougher without raising the shard chance.
  2. Skip white monsters and act bosses. Teleport pack to pack; kill every champion in a champion pack and the boss of a unique pack.
  3. Chain waypoint areas, then remake. For example Lower Kurast → Kurast Bazaar → Upper Kurast → Travincal for Northern, or Frigid Highlands (Eldritch, Shenk) → Anya's portal (Pindleskin) for Western.
  4. Prefer super uniques at fixed spots. They are worth two elite rolls and need no searching.
  5. Check the terror-zone message first. If your target act is terrorized, farm a different shard or go Herald hunting instead.

Build. Maxroll's elite-hunting tier list (2026-05-22) ranks Berserk Barbarian S, the current Echoing Strike Warlock A, and Summoner Warlock B. Echoing Strike needs no change for this. Summoner Warlock (bound Dark Lancer with Cursed and Fanaticism, Goatman, Defiler, Enigma) is the hands-off option at lower speed.
