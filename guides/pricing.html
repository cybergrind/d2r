How to price a D2R item — the method

Written 2026-09-18 for a Softcore / Non-Ladder / PC / Reign of the Warlock player farming Pindleskin, Hell cows and the Anya shop. This page is the procedure: how to go from a tooltip on the ground to “vendor”, “sell for X” or “keep”. The numbers it leans on live in the pricing primer (what is worth what, Traderie asks and diablo2.io fills pulled 2026-09-18); the pickup rules in the farm guide §3; the build's own needs in the Warlock page.

TL;DR — the five questions, in order.
  1. Who pays for this? Value comes from two places only: a runeword recipe that accepts the base, or an S/A-tier build that names the item or affix. If neither exists, stop: vendor.
  2. Does it pass the gate? Every priced item has one or two binary gates: exact socket count, ≥15 % Enhanced Defense/Damage for “Superior”, 45 all-res on a Paladin shield, +2 class skills on a circlet/amulet, 5/5 on a facet, +life on a skiller, 3 sockets on a Void dagger. Fail the gate = floor or vendor, whatever the rest says.
  3. How good is the roll? Past the gate, the roll multiplies price 3–10×: 15 ED vs plain, 45@ vs 40@, +45 life vs +30, 20 FCR vs 10.
  4. What do people ask, and what does it fill for? Read Traderie asks one vote per seller, take the min → median of the matching bucket; expect common rolls to fill near the median and chase rolls at 32–63 % of the ask.
  5. Sell, keep or vendor? Sell ≥ 0.8 Ist (a Mal) singly, 0.2–0.8 only in bulk, below 0.2 vendor; keep currency and anything your build wears.

1 · Step 0 — what the question really is

“Is this worth anything?” means “is there a buyer with runes for exactly this item?”. D2R has no auction house; price is what a specific buyer pays for a specific need. Three facts follow, and they explain almost every verdict on this page:

  • Runewords set base demand. A white Archon Plate is worth something because Enigma / CoH / Fortitude want a 4-socket elite light armor. A white Gothic Plate is worth nothing because no S/A runeword names it. The runeword also fixes the exact socket count — a 3os Monarch is dead, a 4os is Spirit.
  • Builds set affix demand. A Warlock caster amulet sells because three Warlock guides are S tier this season; a +3 Javelin skills white javelin does not because Lightning Fury wants +2 Amazon on top. Demand shifts with the tier list: primer appendix B lists the 26 builds and their key items.
  • Runes are money. Everything is quoted in runes; this guide converts to Ist = 1 (§4). A rune never rolls badly, so it never loses value — which is why the currency runes (Pul and up) are always “keep”.

2 · Step 1 — triage by colour (item on the ground or in the tooltip)

The colour tells you which branch to read. Each branch ends in a verdict or in a pointer to the primer section with the numbers.

Gray or white (normal / superior / ethereal / socketed base)

  1. Is the base class on the runeword list? Elite armor Archon / Dusk / Wire Fleece / Sacred Armor; Mage Plate; Monarch; Sacred Targe / Rondache / Targe; Phase Blade / Berserker Axe / Colossus Blade; the merc polearms and spears (Thresher, Giant Thresher, Cryptic Axe, Great Poleaxe, Colossus Voulge, Mancatcher, Matriarchal Spear); Grand Matron / Matriarchal Bow; Crystal Sword / Flail; Diadem / Tiara; Kite Shield, Bone Shield, Preserved Head; Legend Spike / Fanged Knife / Kris (Void). Not on it → vendor. Normal- and exceptional-tier generic bases are never on it.
  2. Socket count = the runeword's exact count? Yes → continue. 0os → continue only where Larzuk's maximum at the drop's ilvl equals the runeword count (Monarch, Archon, Dusk, Sacred Armor, Sacred Targe, Rondache, Targe, Colossus Voulge → 4; Mage Plate, Kite, Talons, Legend Spike / Fanged Knife / Kris → 3; Bone Shield, Preserved Head → 2); never Larzuk a 0os Giant Thresher / Berserker Axe / Phase Blade for a 4- or 5-socket word — it gets 6. Wrong count (3os Monarch, 5os eth polearm, 1–2os elite armor) → floor, nothing fixes it.
  3. Read the roll. Superior: only ≥15 % ED counts as “Superior” in a price (5–14 % = plain). Ethereal: worth it on merc bases (polearms, Sacred Armor, merc Archon/Dusk), a discount on self-use bases (Spirit, Enigma). Automods: 45@ on Paladin shields (40–44 still sells, <40 floor), +3 Bow skills on Amazon bows, Warlock staff-mods on daggers (§7).
  4. Band → primer §2. Orientation: eth 4os Giant Thresher 5.9 → 11.4 · sup 15ED 0os Archon 11.4 → 22.8 · 45@ Sacred Targe 0os 4 → 40 · plain 4os Monarch / Archon 0.1 → 1 (Low; Larzuk makes every Monarch 4os).

Yellow (rare)

A rare is a lottery of 4–6 affixes; it is worth something only when the slot's must-affixes all landed and one wrong roll did not eat a slot. Check three things before reading affixes:

  • Durability 3× the base? A “failed unique” (the unique was already found this game, or cannot spawn here) — it is an ordinary rare with extra durability, nothing more.
  • Base tier. Rare generic armor / weapons of normal or exceptional tier have no market at all (a rare Razor Bow with 138 ED / 20 IAS / 2os — vendor). Rare elites of generic type sell only with two or more good rolls on a runeword-grade base (Archon, Monarch, Phase Blade…).
  • Slot. Rings, amulets, gloves, boots, belts, circlets and class items are the slots where rares compete with uniques — read them against the table.

Gold (unique) / green (set)

  1. Is it in the primer's unique table (§5)? Yes → read the one roll that matters and take the bucket: Arachnid Mesh ED, Mara's all-res, Crown of Ages sockets + res, Griffon's −res / +dmg, Death's Fathom cold, Titan's eth + ED, Verdungo's vit / PDR, Gheed's MF, War Traveler MF, Andariel's eth 30/10, Hellwarden's Will the two −8 res, Wraithstep the Warlock tree. At threshold → sell at min → median; below → the next bucket down (mostly Um–Ist).
  2. Not in the table → does a build in appendix B name it? Yes → Low tier, sell for a mid rune or wear it (Bloodpact Shard, Entropy Locket for Echoing Strike). No → vendor, except eth merc gear and one of each self-use staple (Stormshield, an IK piece, a Shako).
  3. Set pieces: Tal Rasha's, IK, Trang-Oul's, Aldur's, Griswold's pieces trade at Low–Mid; every RotW set (Bane's, Horazon's) is floor.

Orange (crafted)

Two crafts have a market: Caster Amulet with +2 class skills and 10 FCR from the affix (20 FCR total) — “Jah+” in threads, 2–3 Jah realistic on SC/NL; Blood Gloves with life leech, 5–10 % crushing blow, life and either 20 IAS or +2 skills — Lo … 4 Jah asks. Caster rings with 10 FCR from the affix sell Ist–Vex. Every other craft, including your own caster boots and belts, is self-use or vendor.

Runes, gems, keys, essences, shards, tokens

These are the money. Keep Hel and up and every Perfect gem; price by the ladder (§4) or per unit (primer §6.2: keys ≈ 0.45, P.gems 0.06–0.12, Northern Worldstone Shard 0.17). El–Dol and chipped/flawed/normal gems have no market: cube up 3 → 1 or vendor. Low runes sell only as stacks (“9× Hel → 1 Ist”).

4.3 Asks are not fills

  • One vote per seller. A bucket of 47 listings from 6 sellers is a wall, not a market. Traderie prices[].group entries are OR-alternatives — take the cheapest.
  • Floor rolls fill near the ask (the one observed floor pair filled at 95–118 %). Chase rolls fill at 32–63 % (sup 15ED 6os Phase Blade fill 4.05 vs asks 8.6 → 9.3; sup 15ED 0os Archon fill 7.2 vs 11.4 → 22.8). Quote the median for common rolls, “offer” for chase rolls, never the bucket minimum as “the price”.
  • Stack asks are per stack. An ask of 1 Ist on “Hel ×9” is the whole stack.
  • Snapshot. Every number here is 2026-09-18. Two months later re-pull before quoting a Top-tier item (§5.3).

5 · Step 4 — looking a price up yourself

5.1 Traderie (asks) — the first stop for bases, uniques, charms, jewels

  1. Search the item on traderie.com/diablo2resurrected; open the item page; set Softcore · Non-Ladder · PC, and for bases the rarity (normal / superior / magic) and sockets.
  2. Read the listings newest first. Count sellers, not listings; skip any seller repeated more than twice. Note whether prices are “offer” or fixed.
  3. Write down min → median of the rolls that match yours (same sockets, same ED bracket, same automod). That is the ask band.
  4. Offline / bulk: the repo tools do the same with the SC/NL/PC filters applied client-side and the ladder conversion built in: 
    python3 pricing/tools/traderie.py search "archon plate"
python3 pricing/tools/traderie.py listings <itemId> 4 --sc --nl --pc --props --json
    Item ids used so far: pricing/tools/wp_b_ids.txt.

5.2 diablo2.io (fills and price-checks) — the only source for rares and crafts

  1. Sold rows: forum search with the item keyword, Softcore · Non-Ladder · PC, “active + sold”, sorted by date; read the “Sold for” lines of the last 3 days. Keywords are mandatory; wait ≥ 30 s between searches or you hit the flood page; page 2 is &start=30. 
    python3 pricing/tools/d2io_search.py "archon" irarity=4 imaxsockets=4 ladder=2 hc=2 plat_pc=1 activesold=1 legacy_resu=2 --out pricing/raw/d2io/search-archon.html
python3 pricing/tools/d2io_search.py --parse pricing/raw/d2io/search-archon.html
  2. Price-check threads (“PC: rare ring 104 AR / 8 LL …”): read two or more replies; the answers are stated as a rune range and usually disagree by one tier — take the lower one for SC/NL. The reasoning in those threads is the model for rares: count the affixes that match the slot's buyer, subtract one tier per wrong affix.
  3. Guide bands (the diablo2.io price guide of 2026-08-28) are orientation only — never a price source.

9 · False positives — looks valuable, is not

  • Rare non-elite bows, spears, armors of any roll. Rare elites outside the runeword list unless two must-affixes landed.
  • White javelins / claws / orbs with +3 skills and nothing else; white circlets of any tier.
  • Blue weapons or armor with one affix, however large the number.
  • Superior with 5–14 % ED (it is a plain base with a longer name).
  • Ethereal self-use gear (eth Monarch, eth Archon for yourself) — a discount, not a premium.
  • Finished runewords in self-use bases (Rhyme, Spirit, Stealth) — worth less than the base was.
  • Teleport amulets without +2 class skills; +2 class circlets without FCR.
  • Daggers with Blood Oath / Levitation / Demonic Mastery; grimoires without staff-mods; any Dirk / Dagger / Mithril Point / Bone Knife as a Void base.
  • A rare with 3× durability read as “almost a unique”.

10 · Housekeeping

  • All prices in the guides are a 2026-09-18 snapshot (Traderie asks, diablo2.io fills ≤ 3 days). The method does not age; the numbers do.
  • diablo2.io search spacing: 30 s everywhere (the tools and primer say 30; an older note said 25 — 30 is the safe one).
  • Filters that must always be on: Softcore · Non-Ladder · PC · Reign of the Warlock (Traderie property ids 799 / 800 / 798 / 1854). Ladder and Hardcore prices are a different economy.
  • Absence from the repo is not a price of zero.
