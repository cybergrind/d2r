Preconditions & working assumptions
  • Rule zero (D2 edition): LLM recall of Diablo 2 facts is not evidence. Every number here is copied from a WP-F…I hand-off (feed + n + date) or a cited page (URL + "Last Updated"); anything the packages could not settle is in the §9 verify ledger, not asserted. The game changed in 2026 (Warlock class, grimoires, five new runewords, Terror Zone rework): pre-2026 memory is suspect.
  • Scope = one economy. Softcore · Non-Ladder · PC · Reign of the Warlock. diablo2.io's filter states "Resurrected characters cannot trade with Reign of the Warlock characters and vice versa" (plan, 2026-09-18). Ladder Ist ≠ NL Ist: the d2io guide (2026-08-28) lists Ber 16–22 and Jah 22–35 Ist on Ladder vs 10–12 NL. Traderie property 1854 is a soft filter: 71–98 % of SC/NL/PC rune listings are RotW-tagged (Ber 71 %, every other rune ≥ 83 %; wp-f-ladder.json prop1854_split) and LoD-tagged asks do not price differently at this n (WP-F), so packages kept "unset" and dropped LoD/classic rows.
  • Asks vs fills. Traderie listings and diablo2.io "active" rows are asking prices; the only fills we can fetch are diablo2.io rows marked SOLD in the last 3 days. Recomputed fill/ask pairs (WP-B buckets × WP-G fills, 2026-09-18): Phase Blade 6os sup 15ED fill 4.05 vs asks 8.57 → 9.32 = 43–47 %; Archon 0os sup 15ED fill 7.21 vs 11.42 → 22.84 = 32–63 %; Mage Plate 3os sup <15ED fill 0.79 vs 0.67 → 0.83 = 95–118 % (the floor pair). Rune asks sit 10–25 % above the joint ladder value (Ber/Jah/Zod, wp-f-ladder.json _meta) — Ber ask median 11.4 vs fills 9.3–12 (−5 … +23 %), Jah 13.8 vs fills 10–12 (+15 … +38 %). Fills are thin (1–3 rows per item per 3 days); prefer them where ≥3 exist.
  • Price semantics on Traderie (WP-F, verified on ~1,200 multi-group and 322 stack listings): prices[].group — same group = summed, different groups = OR-alternatives; the effective ask is the cheapest alternative. Rune/gem/key/shard asks are for the whole stack — divide by amount. Non-stackable items (jewels, charms, uniques) read per item. OR-alternative minima can be a seller's throw-away option ("4 Perfect Skull OR 1 Um" → 0.1): medians are robust, minima are not — hence the quoting rule (median or "offer").
  • Single-seller walls. Several Traderie feeds are one seller's inventory: rare Ring 267/268 and rare Amulet 304/323 listings from one seller asking Jah multiples; Zod 41/61 listings from one seller; Mara's 27–29 res 52 listings (46 priced) from 3 sellers; Griffon's 48 listings behind 6 sellers (WP-F, WP-I). WP-I medians are one vote per seller; rare/craft values come from price-check reasoning and fills, never from those feeds.
  • Two claim classes. Mechanics (sockets, Larzuk, ethereal, facet stacking) are stable until the next patch; market numbers are volatile and dated 2026-09-18 — refresh with §7 before acting on a Top-tier decision.
  • Demand weights (WP-A): S-tier build = 2, A-tier = 1, over the 26 S/A builds of the maxroll overall tier list (2026-05-22). "Named by N builds" below means gear-table mentions in those guides (appendix B).
  • Lem. The ladder fixes Lem at 0.20 (guide band midpoint; only 5 rune-for-rune observations, WP-F). WP-I's per-unit stack asks put Lem at 0.40 (n=16, 4 sellers, 9 priced). Every conversion in this file uses 0.20 (all four packages converted with it); §6.2 shows the 0.40 stack figure next to it.
Rejected ideas (and why)
  1. Using maxroll's 2024 value tiers as prices. Both "Valuable" pages are dated 2024-03-06, pre-RotW; the Unique/Set page says its listing "is most accurate at the beginning of a Ladder reset". Used only as category lists and for the "why" text; every number is a 2026-09-18 pull. (plan, Rejected 10)
  2. Reading Traderie stack asks per unit. "Ist ×10 → 1 Jah", "Gul ×7 → 1 Jah" only match the ladder per stack; 322 stack listings confirm it, 15 per-unit-only listings (4 %) were excluded. (WP-F)
  3. Summing a Traderie listing's price groups. The plan first read "2× Jah + 2× Zod + 4× Cham" as one sum; groups are OR-alternatives, so that listing asks the cheapest of them. WP-B's buckets were regenerated with the OR rule (113 stats moved, 87 down). (plan review log, wave 1)
  4. Pricing rares and crafts from the Traderie rare/crafted feeds. They are single-seller walls (267/268 rings, 304/323 amulets, Blood Ring 100/1); recorded as pattern counts, priced from price-check threads and fills instead. (plan review log, wave 2; WP-I)
  5. Quoting chase-roll asks as prices, or bucket minima as prices. Eth 4os sup 15ED Giant Thresher "799", 3os eth sup 15ED Archon "40× Jah", 45@ sup 15ED Sacred Targe 126 are asks with n ≤ 8 and no fills; WP-B measured 3–30× ask inflation on chase rolls vs the guide, and OR-minima can be throw-away options. Chase rolls are "offer" items; common rolls are quoted at the median (TL;DR rule 3). (WP-G; review pass 2)
  6. Traderie per-item price history / stats endpoints. 401 "Unauthorized jwt"; only live asks are available. (plan, Rejected 3)
  7. Automating the diablo2.io price-check form. POST with a per-session sid, rate limit unknown; the 2026-08-28 guide post and GET price-check threads are used instead. (plan, Rejected 4)
  8. Searching Traderie for "colossal ancient". Returns nothing because the catalog names are Defender's Fire/Bile, Protector's Frost/Stone, Guardian's Thunder/Light; those trade (32–76 in-scope asks each). (plan review log, wave 2)
  9. Keyword-less diablo2.io searches, and requests <30 s apart. "No matches found" and empty flood-control pages respectively; always pass a keyword, space calls ≥30 s, retry once. (plan, Rejected 8; review log 9)
  10. Fandom / diablowiki / purediablo forums as sources. 403 behind Cloudflare; not used. (plan, Rejected 5)

1 · Currency — how to read a price

Why runes are the money (maxroll Runes 2026-02-10; Trading 2026-02-10; Rune Value 2024-03-06 for the runeword map). Every rune has a fixed cube exchange rate upward ("2x Ist + Sapphire = Gul … 2x Sur + Flawless Amethyst = Ber … 2x Cham + Flawless Emerald = Zod"). Runes are therefore divisible and fungible, and never lose value to a bad roll. Their demand is set by runewords (observed: "WTB Jah … building my first enigma", "WTB 2 Ber … to make an infinity", WP-F 2026-09-18).

2 · White bases — which are worth a lot, and why

The Larzuk trap — an irreversible mistake. Larzuk gives a base "their maximum possible number of Sockets" for its ilvl (sockets 2026-06-16), and nothing removes sockets. A 0-socket drop is therefore only as good as a socketed one where the base's ilvl-41+ maximum equals the runeword count: Monarch 4 (Spirit), Archon Plate / Dusk Shroud 4 (CoH, Fortitude), Mage Plate 3, Colossus Voulge 4 (Insight), Sacred Targe / Sacred Armor 4. Where the maximum exceeds the runeword — Giant Thresher / Great Poleaxe 6, Thresher / Cryptic Axe / Mancatcher 5 (Infinity and Insight need 4), Berserker Axe 6 (Beast/Grief need 5), Grand Matron Bow 5 (Faith 4), Phase Blade 6 (Grief 5), Archon Plate 4 (Enigma 3) — a 0os base Larzuk'd becomes a 6/5-socket base worth only what BotD/Obedience/Last Wish buyers pay (eth 0os sup 15ED GT asks 9.3 → 10.4 vs 4os 57 → 86). Never Larzuk those; cube a normal 0os one (a lottery, 16.67 % for 4 on a max-5/6 base) or sell it as 0os.

The cause, in one table (WP-G, 2026-09-18). A white base is tradeable when an S/A-tier runeword recipe accepts it and the drop has the exact socket count Larzuk/cube cannot manufacture; the roll then multiplies the price. Ranking = median ask of the best clean-white bucket with n ≥ 3 priced (WP-B, 2026-09-18; Crystal Sword's and Colossus Voulge's chase buckets have only 2 and 1 priced — footnoted); "w" = WP-A demand weight (guide naming, S=2/A=1).
