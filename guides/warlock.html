Echoing Strike Warlock — gear, crafts, Warlock items, loot filter

D2R Reign of the Warlock · Softcore / Non-Ladder / PC · farming Pindleskin, Hell cows and the Anya shop. Written 2026-09-18. Companion pages: Pindle & Anya farm guide (run loop, pickup rules, base prices) · pricing primer (what is worth what) · how to price an item.

TL;DR. The build is a caster that hits with a dagger: Void Legend Spike, Spirit or Lidless off-hand, Enigma or Chains of Honor, a +2 Warlock circlet or Shako, Trang-Oul's or Magefist gloves, Arachnid Mesh or a caster belt, SoJ / BK / caster rings, a +2 Warlock 10-FCR amulet. Three cube crafts belong to it: Caster Boots, Caster Belt, Blood Ring; Caster Amulet and Caster Ring are the same-slot upgrades the guide lists as “rare”. Every craft needs a blue base of a specific type (§3) plus a low rune, a perfect gem and any jewel. The loot filter lootfilter/warlock_lean.json (§6) already shows the bases, jewels, rings and amulets those recipes eat. Your Natalya's Soul boots (40 FRW, two resists) are fine until an Enigma makes run speed irrelevant.

Breakpoints (maxroll Echoing Strike guide, Aug 26, 2026). FCR: 75 % is the starter target (Spirit gets you there), 125 % is the max breakpoint the Standard and Magic Find setups hold. FHR: at least 26 %. Only the 75 and 125 thresholds change the cast animation; anything between them is wasted. Wizardspike (50 FCR) vs Spirit (25–35 FCR): the swap only pays if it crosses 125.

Stat points (same guide). Warlock starts 15 str / 20 dex / 25 vit / 20 energy; 3 life per vitality point. Strength only as far as the gear needs (Spirit Monarch 156, Legend Spike 65, Wizardspike 32). Dexterity: 50 points for the Starter setup, ~100 for Standard and Magic Find (attack rating — Echoing Strike is a weapon hit and can miss; Wizardspike needs 63 dex, Legend Spike 67, Fanged Knife 86). Energy none. Everything else Vitality.

Skills (same guide). 1 point each: Echoing Strike, Sigil: Lethargy, Summon Defiler. Then max in order: Hex Purge → Mirrored Blades → Hex Bane → Demonic Mastery (Starter stops at 5, Standard 10) → Eldritch Blast → Blood Oath. Starter puts spare points into Consume for duration instead of Eldritch Blast / Blood Oath. Echoing Strike only applies Hex Purge; Hex Purge is the damage.

Rotation (same guide). Buffs, cast on yourself and recast when they expire: Battle Command twice, then Battle Orders (CtA swap); two Defilers (three at Demonic Mastery 10), recast BC/BO so they inherit the level; Consume one Defiler for the Magic Damage % buff; Hex Purge; Eldritch Blast. Hex Purge is a weapon hex — every successful Echoing Strike hit applies it to the target, it detonates as AoE magic damage up to 3 times, then the next hit reapplies it. Eldritch Blast runs around the Warlock on its own. Per pack there are only two targeted casts: Sigil: Lethargy under elites and bosses (lowers their defense), then Echoing Strike. Cursor close to the character widens the blade fan for packs; far away narrows it into one line for bosses or corridors, and all five blades can hit one target on the way out and back. Blade Warp between packs until Enigma replaces it with Teleport. Immunities are irrelevant (physical + magic split); Renewed Black Cleft handles the few magic immunes.

3.1 Item level — why where the base comes from matters

  • Crafted ilvl = ⌊clvl/2⌋ + ⌊base ilvl/2⌋. The number of random affixes is rolled from that ilvl: at crafted ilvl ≥ 71 you get the full 1–4 (each extra affix a coin-flip chain), lower ilvl caps you at fewer. Craft on a level 90+ character with a Hell-dropped base (Pindle's pack is mlvl 86, cows 81–ish in Hell) and you are always past the cap.
  • Affix level = ilvl − qlvl/2 gates which affixes can appear. +2 class skills on an amulet needs a high-level amulet, 30 FRW on boots needs a high-level base. Hell Anya's stock is ilvl = clvl + 5 (cap 99), so at clvl 90 she sells ilvl-95 magic rings, amulets, boots and belts — the best craft bases you can buy, and repeatable every Pindle run (farm guide §4).
  • Gambled rings/amulets also work but cost ~50k gold each; shop bases cost a few thousand.

5 · Pickup rules that matter for this build (full list: farm guide §3)

  • Gray or Superior bases only from the runeword list: Archon / Dusk / Wire Fleece / Sacred Armor (4os), Mage Plate (3), Monarch (4), every Sacred Targe / Rondache / Targe (read all-res), Phase Blade / Berserker Axe / Colossus Blade, the seven eth merc polearms and spears, Grand Matron / Matriarchal Bow, Crystal Sword / Flail for self-use, 2os Bone Shield / Preserved Head (Rhyme), Legend Spike / Fanged Knife / Kris (Void).
  • Every unique, set, rune, flawless+ gem, charm, jewel, ring, amulet.
  • ID every magic or rare circlet-class helm, class item (claws, orbs, Amazon weapons, Paladin shields, Barb helms, pelts, Necro heads, Warlock daggers/grimoires), Jeweler's elite armor/Monarch, rare gloves / boots / belts.
  • Leave: white or magic generic armor/weapons of any tier, rare non-elite generic armor/weapons, rare elites outside the runeword list, chipped/flawed/normal gems, El–Dol singles, plain magic Large Charms.
  • A rare with triple durability is a failed unique: nothing more.
